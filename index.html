ChronoCast-1 / zero-shot time-series foundation model

2.4B params · decoder-only context: anything you can draw

Forecast anything. Draw a squiggle, paste some numbers — get a principled continuation with calibrated uncertainty.

ChronoCast-1 is a decoder-only foundation model pretrained on 4 trillion synthetic and real time-series tokens. In this live demo it runs entirely in your browser: sketch any series on the canvas (or paste one as text), press Run forecast, and the model decodes the next 64 steps with 10–90% predictive bands. (It is actually trend + seasonality decomposition with a residual bootstrap, in a trench coat.)
